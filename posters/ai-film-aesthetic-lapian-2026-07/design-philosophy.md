# Cinematic Ledger

Cinematic Ledger treats a course schedule as a professional viewing log: dark, precise, and built for quick trust. The final work should feel meticulously crafted, with every row, label, divider, and spacing decision adjusted through painstaking attention. It is not an illustrated poster; it is a high-confidence training artifact where the table itself carries the visual authority.

The visual system is nocturnal and cinematic, using deep navy as the screening room and controlled blue, orange, pink, green, and yellow signals as production annotations. Color is informational first: live lessons, homework days, submission moments, and graduation critique each receive their own restrained badge. The result should show master-level execution through clarity rather than ornament.

Space is built around the rhythm of watching, analyzing, generating, and assembling. Repeated rows become frames; dates become timecode; the teacher column behaves like a slate marker; the final row closes the sequence like an end card. The composition should look labored over with care, as if someone at the top of their field refined it for a public course announcement.

Typography stays compact and authoritative. The title names the camp directly, the date range anchors the training period, and the table keeps all lesson content readable in one square frame. Text is essential and integrated into the structure, never decorative filler. Alignment, baseline discipline, and breathing room are the craft.

The subtle reference is professional film analysis: the poster should feel like a dark-room breakdown sheet transformed into a polished public course graphic.
