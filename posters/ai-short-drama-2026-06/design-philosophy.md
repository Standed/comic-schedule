# Schedule Noir

Schedule Noir treats a course schedule as a polished product screenshot: precise, quiet, and immediately usable. The work should feel meticulously crafted, as if every row height, label color, and baseline has been adjusted by someone who has shipped real course materials under pressure. Space is organized around trust: a restrained title area, a clear brand mark, and a dense table that carries nearly all meaning.

The palette is nocturnal but practical. Deep navy, cool blue, orange, pink, green, and yellow create a professional training-camp atmosphere where every accent has a job. Color is an information system rather than decoration: live sessions, homework days, review moments, submission points, and graduation feedback each receive a distinct signal. The final canvas must show master-level execution through disciplined restraint, not visual noise.

Form is modular and table-led. Repeated rows, light dividers, subtle highlight bands, and compact badges preserve the original Base schedule's shape while turning it into a clean public-facing poster. The schedule is not over-illustrated; it is clarified. Rhythms alternate between live teaching and output days so the viewer feels the pulse of making, revising, and shipping.

Typography is sparse and authoritative. Large type names the camp, small metadata gives date context, and table text stays compact enough for all 18 lessons to remain readable in one square frame. No long explanations are needed. Essential words are integrated into the visual architecture with painstaking attention to proportion, baseline, and breathing room.

The finished poster should look labored over with care: crisp edges, exact alignment, clear hierarchy, and no accidental overlaps. Its conceptual thread is the transformation from an internal Base table into a public course schedule graphic. Anyone familiar with the original table should recognize the schedule DNA immediately; everyone else should simply see a polished, high-trust course poster.
